Privacy Policy

Daybook for iPhone · Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version

We do not collect your data. Daybook has no accounts, no login and no server of ours. Your photographs, notes, project details and locations are stored on your device, and — if you turn on iCloud sync — in your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. We cannot see any of it.

What Daybook stores, and where

WhatWhere it is kept
Photographs and their originalsOn your device; in your private iCloud if sync is on
Capture time, GPS coordinate, device model, dimensionsAttached to each photograph, in the same places
Project details, client names, notesSame
Your subscription statusWith Apple, not with us

Nothing in that table is transmitted to us or to any third party.

Location

If you grant location access, Daybook records where each photograph was taken so the photo can prove where it happened — the point of an evidence record. The coordinate is stored with the photograph and is never sent anywhere. You can decline location access and everything else in the app keeps working.

Camera, microphone and speech

The camera is used to take photographs into your projects. The microphone is used only when you record a voice caption, and that recording is transcribed on your device — the audio does not leave it. Text recognition on photographs also runs entirely on your device.

Network requests

Daybook makes one kind of network request of its own: a request to apple.com whose response header gives the current time, so the app can tell you whether your device's clock is accurate. It sends no information about you or your work.

If you enable iCloud sync or iCloud file backup, your device talks to Apple's iCloud service on your behalf, under Apple's privacy policy, using your own account and your own storage.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active.

Your data stays yours

Daybook does not hold your evidence hostage. Everything you have captured remains readable and exportable on any plan, including after a subscription lapses — as a PDF report, or as a ZIP of every original photograph with a manifest of its hashes and locations.

To delete your data, delete the projects or photographs in the app, or delete the app. If you use iCloud sync, you can also remove Daybook's data from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Children

Daybook is a tool for working professionals and is not directed at children.
