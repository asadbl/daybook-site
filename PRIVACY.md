# Daybook — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 August 2026_

Daybook is a field-documentation app. It records photographs, notes and locations for the jobs
you work on. This policy describes what happens to that material.

## The short version

**We do not collect your data.** Daybook has no accounts, no login and no server of ours. Your
photographs, notes, project details and locations are stored on your device, and — if you turn on
iCloud sync — in your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. We cannot see any of
it.

## What Daybook stores, and where

| What | Where it is kept |
|---|---|
| Photographs and their originals | On your device; in your private iCloud if sync is on |
| Capture time, GPS coordinate, device model, dimensions | Attached to each photograph, in the same places |
| Project details, client names, notes | Same |
| Your subscription status | With Apple, not with us |

Nothing in that table is transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Location

If you grant location access, Daybook records where each photograph was taken so the photo can
prove where it happened — the point of an evidence record. The coordinate is stored with the
photograph and is never sent anywhere. You can decline location access and everything else in the
app keeps working.

## Camera, microphone and speech

The camera is used to take photographs into your projects. The microphone is used only when you
record a voice caption, and that recording is transcribed **on your device** — the audio does not
leave it. Text recognition on photographs also runs entirely on your device.

## Network requests

Daybook makes one kind of network request of its own: a request to `apple.com` whose response
header gives the current time, so the app can tell you whether your device's clock is accurate.
It sends no information about you or your work.

If you enable iCloud sync or iCloud file backup, your device talks to Apple's iCloud service on
your behalf, under Apple's privacy policy, using your own account and your own storage.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple
tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active.

## Your data stays yours

Daybook does not hold your evidence hostage. Everything you have captured remains readable and
exportable on any plan, including after a subscription lapses — as a PDF report, or as a ZIP of
every original photograph with a manifest of its hashes and locations.

To delete your data, delete the projects or photographs in the app, or delete the app; if you use
iCloud sync, you can also remove Daybook's data from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

## Children

Daybook is a tool for working professionals and is not directed at children.
